# Adeyemi's Ascent

Build a premium, fully responsive personal portfolio website for **Adeyemi Anibi**, also known professionally as **Larry Anibi**.

The website should position him as a high-potential **Sales, Retail and Customer Experience Professional** with proven customer-facing experience and clear readiness to progress into **team leadership, supervision, assistant management and store management**.

The portfolio must feel confident, authoritative and commercially strong. It should communicate that Adeyemi is dependable, adaptable, results-oriented and capable of making an immediate contribution to an employer.

The website should not feel like a generic CV template. It should feel like a polished professional brand built for a candidate targeting competitive UK employers.

## Primary positioning

Use this professional positioning throughout the website:

**Sales, Retail and Customer Experience Professional**

Secondary positioning:

**Customer Service | Retail Operations | Sales Performance | Team Support | Leadership Potential**

The portfolio should demonstrate that Adeyemi:

* Understands customers
* Resolves problems calmly
* Performs well under pressure
* Supports sales and commercial goals
* Works effectively with teams
* Learns new systems quickly
* Can take on increased responsibility
* Has the potential to progress into management

Do not falsely describe him as an Assistant Manager or Store Manager. Present him as a strong frontline professional with demonstrated leadership potential and readiness for increased responsibility.
